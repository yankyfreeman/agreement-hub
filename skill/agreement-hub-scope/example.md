## 2. What we are building

An online booking system for the clinic: patients book appointments on the
website, get reminders by SMS, and staff manage the calendar from an admin
panel.

**Not included:** patient medical records, insurance claims, a mobile app.

## 3. Patient Website

### 3.1 Booking an appointment

1. The patient picks a treatment (check-up, cleaning, whitening, emergency).
2. They pick a dentist, or "any dentist".
3. They choose a free time slot (see Q1).
4. They enter their name, phone number and email.
5. They get a confirmation on screen and by email.

### 3.2 Changing or cancelling

- The confirmation email has a link to change or cancel the appointment.
- Changes are allowed until 24 hours before the appointment (see Q3).

## 4. SMS Reminders

- A reminder is sent 24 hours before each appointment.
- The patient can reply **C** to confirm or **X** to cancel.

> SMS messages are paid per message to the SMS provider (see Q4).

## 5. Admin Panel

### 5.1 Calendar

- Day and week view per dentist.
- Staff can add, move and cancel appointments.

### 5.2 Opening hours and time off

- Set opening hours for each day of the week.
- Block time off for a dentist (holidays, training).
